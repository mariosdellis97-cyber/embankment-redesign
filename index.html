Our Story

Late-Victorian character, modern comfort.

Built in 1891 and set on the banks of the River Great Ouse, The Embankment has been restored to reflect its late-Victorian heyday, timber gables, tall sash windows and all, while adding every comfort a modern stay or a long lunch actually needs.

Today it's one of Bedford's most-loved spots for relaxed dining, weekend stays, celebrations and private events.

Stay

Boutique rooms, river views.

Each room pairs period character, bay windows, high ceilings, the odd chandelier, with the comfort you'd expect from a proper stay: a well-dressed bed, a good shower and somewhere quiet to actually switch off.
